Tygris — what the capture pipeline could grow into

Everything below reads from the same underlying capture events (camera, timestamp, media, ReID candidates) — each module is a different lens on that one stream, not a separate system.

Exists today

Single-shot identify
Upload one photo, get a candidate match. No history kept.
Tiger catalogue & dossier
Browse enrolled tigers, per-tiger capture history.
Captures — review queue
Ambiguous matches and conflicts needing a human call, merged with camera-network health (was two separate pages: Attention Queue, Station Health).
Captures — Capture Log
Real, persisted photo per sighting; paginated, camera-filterable browsable history — the "next" item below, now built.
Pass-1 video screening
One-off: sample a clip, flag candidate frames.

Planned — next next

Pairs & Family Groups
Courtship, mother-cub litters, territory overlap between known tigers.
Review-resolve → embedding gallery
Enrolling a new individual from the review queue doesn't yet hot-load into the live re-ID matcher's gallery.

Natural extensions now that events are persisted

Territory & movement map
Per-tiger range over time, heatmap of zone activity, corridor usage.
Camera health board
Offline/battery-low/no-pickup cameras — ops, not ecology.
Population census
Live count by age/sex, new cubs this season, mortality/disappearance flags.
Breeding season calendar
Expected windows vs. observed courtship events, gestation countdowns.
Injury / health flags
Visible wounds, limping, poor body condition noted on a sighting.
Human-wildlife conflict log
Livestock-kill or boundary-crossing sightings routed to a different alert path.
Reviewer audit trail
Who confirmed/rejected which event, when — for compliance reporting.
Forest-dept export
Scheduled PDF/CSV census & sighting reports in the department's format.
